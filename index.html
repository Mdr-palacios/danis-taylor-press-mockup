About the author

Mildred Danis-Taylor is a wife, mother, organizer, and now author. When her husband Rodney — a Georgia barber and double amputee — was detained by ICE and held at Stewart Detention Center, she became one of his loudest advocates, speaking with members of Congress, city councils, and anyone who would listen.

“Write a short quote from the book or from Mildred here.”

After more than 15 months, Rodney came home in May 2026. Her book tells what those months were really like — and what families facing detention need to know. Mildred works with the Black Alliance for Just Immigration (BAJI), building power for Black immigrants and their families.
